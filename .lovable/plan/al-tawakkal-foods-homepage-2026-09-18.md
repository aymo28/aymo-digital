# Al Tawakkal Foods homepage

## Goal
Build one polished homepage that follows the supplied reference’s overall structure and visual rhythm, so the theme can be reviewed before the rest of the site is created.

## Page structure
- Slim promotional strip and clear navigation
- Bold agency introduction with primary and secondary actions
- Custom marketing visual with performance highlights
- Trust/partner row
- Six service cards
- Dark results band with key numbers
- Client testimonial
- Three marketing insight cards
- Newsletter signup and compact footer

## Visual direction
- White canvas with deep navy text and a vivid violet accent
- Modern, confident typography with tight hierarchy
- Crisp cards, fine borders, restrained shadows, and small purposeful motion
- Fully responsive layout for desktop and mobile
- The uploaded screenshot will be used only as design reference, not displayed in the site
