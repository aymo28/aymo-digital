# **Aymo Digital — Master Website Expansion & Improvement Specification**

## **1. Core instruction**

Improve and expand the existing Aymo Digital website.

**DO NOT rebuild the website from scratch.**

**DO NOT remove the existing approved homepage sections, visual identity, animations, desktop theme, mobile responsiveness, or existing content unless specifically instructed below.**

The existing homepage is already approved and should remain recognizable.

The goal is to make it significantly more useful, trustworthy, conversion-focused, SEO-friendly, and complete while preserving the current slate/glass visual direction.

Think of this as:

> **Existing Aymo Digital website + a complete client decision-making layer**

The website should answer every major question a potential client has before contacting the agency:

- What does Aymo do?
- Can Aymo help a business like mine?
- What service do I actually need?
- What exactly will I get?
- How does the process work?
- What happens after I contact Aymo?
- How long does the process take?
- What does working with Aymo look like?
- What results/proof exist?
- How is performance measured?
- How much does it cost / what is the starting investment?
- How can I contact Aymo?
- Can I book directly?
- Can I request a quotation?
- Why should I trust this company?

Do not solve these questions by adding unnecessary long paragraphs. Use clear sections, cards, comparisons, numbers, timelines, FAQs, case studies, and interactive elements.
